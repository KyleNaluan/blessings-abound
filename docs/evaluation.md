## How the rules were tuned

- Games and hardware use an ask-to-sale factor of 0.94, which is one over the 1.06 median asking price per reference on the tuning items.
- A 95% range on that median runs from 0.84 to 1.87, so 18 items can't pin the factor down and it stays an assumption.
- Games and hardware use a range from the 15th to the 85th percentile, in place of the 25th to 75th.
- With the tuned factor, the 25th to 75th percentiles held 10 of the 18 references, about what a band holding half the asking prices would hold.
- The 20th to 80th held 11, the 15th to 85th held 15, and the 10th to 90th held 16 at about 1.4 times the width, so the 15th to 85th was the choice.
- A wider range holds more references by being less precise, which is why each table shows the width and the pipeline at the baseline's percentiles.
- Confidence still reads the 25th to 75th percentiles, and the comp filter and searches were not changed.
- Apparel keeps the factor of 0.645 and the 25th to 75th percentiles, and no apparel result was looked at or used.
- The fresh list was committed before its first score, which `git log -- api/eval/fresh_items.json` shows, and the rules were not changed after it.

## How it is scored

- The pipeline is the vision model's item card, unedited, passed through `price_item`.
- The baseline is the 25th, 50th and 75th percentile of every listing on the first search's page, with no filter, trim or ask-to-sale factor.
- An item is in range when its reference price falls between the low and the high.
- An item with no price counts as out of range, and median error covers priced items.
- Error is the distance from the typical price to the reference, as a percent of the reference.
- Both sets of games and hardware were fixed before their first run.

## Limits

- These are small sets, so one or two items can move each number.
- Only 3 fresh items are games, all old cartridges, because few openly licensed photos show a named game, so the result says little about discs or newer games.
- PriceCharting's prices come from completed eBay sales through its own calculation, so they are sold prices from the same marketplace as the comps, not from another market.
- Whether PriceCharting's prices include shipping was not checked, and comps omit it.
- A loose console can differ from a listing in what comes with it, such as a controller.
- Most photos are clean studio shots on white, which are easier than a thrift store's.
- PriceCharting's prices are cited here for scoring only, and the app never reads them.
- Live searches here go through the saved responses, so they do not add `comp_searches` rows and are not counted against `SERPAPI_MONTHLY_BUDGET`.
